Power rule, geometrically

Nudge x by dx: the square gains two strips (2x·dx), the cube gains three slabs (3x²·dx)
grows like dx(dx)² and smaller
The red pieces grow in proportion to dx; the grey leftovers are (dx)² or smaller. Shrink dx and ΔA/dx approaches 2x (ΔV/dx approaches 3x²) — that is the power rule.
