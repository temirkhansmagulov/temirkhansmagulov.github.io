ε–δ limits

lim f(x) = L means: for every ε > 0 there is a δ > 0 that keeps f(x) inside the ε-band
Shrink ε, then press “Find a δ”. If L is the true limit, a δ always exists. Change L to a wrong value — or pick the jump or sin(1/x) — and eventually no δ works.
