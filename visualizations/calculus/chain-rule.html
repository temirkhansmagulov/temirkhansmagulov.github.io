Chain rule on number lines

A nudge dx is stretched by g′ on the way to u, then by f′ on the way to y
Each map stretches small intervals by its derivative: du ≈ g′(x)·dx and dy ≈ f′(u)·du. So dy/dx = f′(g(x))·g′(x). Shrink dx and the measured ratio matches the product.
