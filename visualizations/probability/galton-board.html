Galton board

Each ball bounces left or right at every row — the bins fill up into a binomial, then a bell curve
A ball's bin = number of right-bounces = a binomial B(n, p) count. Drop a few hundred balls: the bars approach the expected counts (dots). Change p to tilt the board — the bell shifts but keeps its shape.
