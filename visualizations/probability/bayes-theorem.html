Bayes' theorem

1000 people take a test. Among those who test positive, how many are actually sick?
sick, tests +healthy, tests + (false alarm)sick, tests −healthy, tests −
With a rare disease, most positives come from the huge healthy group — even with an accurate test. Press “Test positive again”: the first result becomes the new prior, and a second positive raises the probability sharply.
