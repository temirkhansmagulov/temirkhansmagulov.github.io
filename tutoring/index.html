About me

Your photo

I've been tutoring since 2018 — first high school students, and since last year university students taking core math courses. Over those years I've learned where students usually get stuck, and that memorising steps doesn't get them unstuck — understanding does.

So every lesson has two parts: first we break the topic down until the idea behind it is clear, then we solve problems together. I don't just show solutions; I ask questions that lead you to find the next step yourself, so by the end you can solve problems on your own — in the exam, too.

Policies

Simple rules so lessons run smoothly for everyone.

Individual lessons

  • Payment: before each lesson.
  • Rescheduling & cancellation: free with at least 24 hours' notice.
  • Late cancellation or no-show: charged in full. Once per semester I'll waive this for a genuine emergency.
  • Being late: the lesson still ends at the scheduled time.
  • If I cancel: you choose a new time or get a full refund.

Group lessons

  • Payment: the month is paid upfront, before its first lesson.
  • Minimum size: a group starts with at least 5 students; if it doesn't fill, full refund.
  • Missed lessons: not refunded, but you get all the materials and the Moodle quiz to catch up.
  • Refunds: full refund before the first lesson; once the month starts it isn't refundable — you can simply not renew.
  • If I cancel: the lesson is rescheduled or credited to your next month.
  • Attendance: come with homework done. Missing 3 lessons in a row without notice may give your seat to the waitlist.

06

Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to see the answer.

Is the trial lesson really free?

Yes. It's 30 minutes, with no obligation to continue. We talk about your course and goals, do a short diagnostic, and I suggest a plan.

I'm really behind. Is that okay?

Yes. The trial lesson and the first quizzes show where the gaps are, and we start from there — even if that's material from earlier courses.

Can you help with my specific university course?

Yes. Send me your syllabus, lecture notes or past exams and I'll build the lessons around them.

How many lessons will I need?

It depends on your goal. For ongoing support, one lesson a week usually works well. Before an exam, students often take two or three a week.

Individual or group — which should I choose?

Individual lessons are fully tailored to you and your schedule. Groups follow a set plan at a lower price per hour and suit students taking a standard course who can attend at a fixed time.

What do I need for the lessons?

A laptop or tablet with Google Meet, a stable internet connection, and something to write on. Moodle works in your browser.

How do I pay?

Payment methods (e.g. Kaspi transfer, card)

Can you do my homework or take my exam for me?

No. I'll help you understand the material well enough to do it yourself.

Do you teach high school math or SAT prep?

No — I currently teach university-level math only.
